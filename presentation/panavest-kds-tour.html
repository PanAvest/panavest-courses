Step 1

Login Or Create An Account

The platform gives returning learners a direct sign-in path and gives new users a short guided registration flow.

How to use it

  1. Open the website and click Sign in.
  2. Choose Google sign-in or use email and password.
  3. New users can switch to Create your account and complete the guided sign-up steps.
The sign-up flow collects full name, date of birth, education level, country, email, and password before account activation.

Step 2

Browse Knowledge Programs

Learners browse the Knowledge catalog, open a course page, and see the description, pricing, CPPD points, and the Enroll (Mobile Money/Card) action.

How to use it

  1. Open Knowledge from the main navigation.
  2. Browse the available programs and choose one.
  3. Open the course detail page to review the summary and decide whether to sign in, enroll, or start learning.
Note: Course detail pages show pricing, bundled access context, and the enrollment action before checkout.

Step 3

Payment Flow

Paid course enrollment supports Mobile Money and card payments. E-book purchases use the in-app checkout flow shown here.

How to use it

  1. Select a paid course or e-book after signing in.
  2. Review the item, amount, and payment method.
  3. Complete payment and return to the product page or dashboard with access unlocked.

Step 4

E-Books Catalog And Reader Access

The e-book flow is direct: browse the catalog, open a title, review the price, and sign in to buy or unlock reading access.

How to use it

  1. Open E-Books from the top navigation.
  2. Select a title to view the cover, summary, and access controls.
  3. Buy the title or open the reader when access is available.
The detail page keeps reading and purchase actions in the same place so the learner does not need extra steps.

Step 5

Dashboard

Once the learner is signed in, the dashboard becomes the control center for progress, purchased content, and certificate access.

How to use it

  1. Sign in and open Dashboard.
  2. Review enrolled knowledge programs and progress updates.
  3. Open purchased e-books and reach certificates from the same page.
The dashboard also stores the learner name used on certificates and keeps course and e-book access in one place.

Step 6

Certificates And Verification

After course completion and assessment success, the learner can preview, download, and verify a certificate with a reference number and QR area.

How to use it

  1. Complete the course and pass the final assessment.
  2. Open the certificates area from the dashboard.
  3. Preview, download, or share the verification link and certificate number.
The certificate preview includes the learner name, course title, issue date, certificate number, and a verification QR section.
